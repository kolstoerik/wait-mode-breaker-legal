# Privacy Policy for Wait-Mode Breaker

**Last updated:** October 2026

Wait-Mode Breaker ("the App") is developed and operated by Kolstø Development ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use Wait-Mode Breaker.

### 1. Information We Collect
- **Account Information:** When you register an account, we may collect your email address and authentication credentials via our backend provider (Supabase).
- **App Usage & User Data:** Tasks, timers, context settings, and completion statuses created within the App to deliver the core service.
- **Payment Data:** If you purchase subscriptions or premium features, transactions are processed securely via third-party payment processors (e.g., Stripe, Google Play In-App Billing). We do not store full credit card numbers or financial details on our servers.

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected data solely to:
- Provide, maintain, and improve the functionality of the App.
- Sync your settings and micro-tasks across devices.
- Process transactions and manage your account.
- Prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations.

### 3. Data Storage & Security
Your data is securely stored using industry-standard cloud infrastructure with end-to-end encryption in transit. We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to provide the service or until you request account deletion.

### 4. Third-Party Services
We may utilize trusted third-party providers to facilitate our service:
- **Authentication & Database:** Supabase
- **Payments:** Stripe / Google Play Billing
These third parties have access to your personal information only to perform specific tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

### 5. Your Rights & Data Deletion
Under applicable privacy laws (including GDPR), you have the right to access, rectify, or request the deletion of your personal data. You can delete your account and associated task data directly within the app settings or by contacting us at our support email.

### 6. Children's Privacy
Wait-Mode Breaker is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.
